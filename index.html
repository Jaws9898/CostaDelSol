About Us

I've spent years behind the counter in busy coffee shops, learning how to serve great coffee and keep standards high day after day. After years of talking about it, I finally made the move to Spain and turned the idea into something real. My journey was documented on the Channel 4 TV show A New Life in the Sun, and the support from viewers who've come to find the café has been incredible.

Cafe del Sol has now been open for over a year, and it's grown faster than I ever expected thanks to a steady stream of regulars, visitors, and word-of-mouth reviews. Since opening, I've been improving the café nonstop—upgrading, tweaking, and pushing through the usual challenges that come with building something from scratch in a new country.

I wanted to create a friendly local spot where people come back not just for the coffee, but for the welcome. That's what Cafe del Sol is all about, and I can't wait to see you here.

— James
