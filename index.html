About Us

James has spent years behind the counter in busy coffee shops, learning how to serve great coffee and keep standards high day after day. After years of talking about it, he made the move to Spain and turned the idea into something real, with his journey documented on the TV show A Life in the Sun.

Cafe del Sol has now been open for over a year, and it's grown fast thanks to a steady stream of regulars, visitors, and word-of-mouth reviews. Since opening, James has kept improving the café nonstop—upgrading, tweaking, and pushing through the usual challenges that come with building something from scratch.

The result is a friendly local spot where people come back not just for the coffee, but for the welcome.
